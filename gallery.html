Our Story

Mabirizi Complex, Kampala

Where every woman deserves to feel beautiful

Hero Moses Hair Salon was founded with one simple belief that every woman who walks through our doors deserves to leave feeling transformed, confident, and utterly beautiful.

Located at Level 4, Room 22 of Mabirizi Complex in the heart of Kampala, we offer a premium, upscale experience at prices that celebrate every woman not just a few. From everyday styling to bridal transformations, our team of expert stylists brings skill, care, and artistry to every single appointment.

We are open 6 days a week. Walk-ins are always welcome, appointments are recommended on weekends and for special services.
